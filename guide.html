Scenario planning

Sign in with the project account used by your practice. Your Ekwo Cloud sign-in code is separate. This interface opens your existing accounts and cases; it does not change the Ekwo UI.

Review alternatives

Choose a company and date range. Select one scenario in each case to compare its contribution with the baseline. Choices are saved for your user; other users keep their own selections. Use baseline clears your choices for the displayed company.

Account balances run through the end date. The book-result card uses movements between the chosen dates. Open an account to inspect up to 500 selected lines. Differences only hides unchanged balances.

Inspect and copy a batch

Inspect / copy batch opens its journals and lines. Copy alternative creates a new draft scenario in the same case. Copy reference preserves an immutable copy of the intended entries before posting; it does not create another selectable scenario. Saved references are accessible from the original batch.

Post a candidate

After copying an unchanged reference, reopen the original candidate and choose a posting cutoff. Post candidate asks for confirmation before adding due draft journals to the shared baseline. Future journals remain drafts. Repeating the same operation does not create additional actuals.

Once actuals exist, their applied contribution stays attached to its case. A private alternative replaces that contribution only in the comparison. Selecting the applied scenario shows its posted actuals; copy it as an alternative to include its remaining future drafts.

Read the accounting basis

Case assumptions describe the reconstruction and projection basis. Unresolved settlements, assessments and other source gaps remain unresolved. Known cash is not necessarily available liquidity. Scenario figures are incremental accounting consequences, not a complete trading forecast or a calculated tax saving.

Continue in Ekwo

Open Ekwo opens the existing accounting application in another tab. Ordinary capture and reports remain there. Lustre provides the separate scenario comparison and inspector.

Appearance and usage

The accent control switches cobalt and gold; the day/night control switches themes. These choices stay in this browser. Usage record downloads operation names, timings and success for this page session, without accounting contents or credentials. Sign out clears the local authentication session.
