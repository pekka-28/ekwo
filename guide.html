Cases

Sign in with your project account, then select a company. The company list comes from your authorised database access.

Cases and scenarios

Cases opens the planning workspace. Create a case, edit its name and notes, and create or copy scenarios. Each scenario references a transaction batch. Copy can create another scenario or preserve an immutable reference batch.

Status and selection

Choose a status directly: Proposal, Reviewing, Accepted, Applied or Retired. These are editable labels in this version, without transition rules or an approval workflow. Changing a label does not post entries or change the case's actual accounting contribution.

The Selected control saves your private scenario choice for that case. Other cases and other users retain their choices. Use baseline clears your private choice for the current case.

Accounting in Ekwo

All accounting menu items and Transactions links open native Ekwo in another tab for the selected company. Native Ekwo controls its own sign-in and accounting-period selection. Transactions opens the company's chart of accounts; native Ekwo does not currently provide a Lustre batch filter. Native reports continue to show the ordinary ledger.

Appearance

The accent and day/night controls change this browser's appearance. Sign out clears the displayed company and case information.
