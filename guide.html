Cases

Sign in with your project account, then select a company. The company list comes from your authorised database access.

Access

Cases and Forecasts require Planning access. Accounting-only users see Reports for their authorised company. Open retrieves a completed historical document; it cannot generate, rebuild or recalculate one. Native accounting links retain authoritative results for an accounting-only identity. Planning users can use a separate accounting identity when they need that guarantee.

Cases and scenarios

Cases opens the planning workspace. Case details holds the shared background and assumptions. Scenario details holds the proposal and assumptions for that alternative. Both provide large description fields with paragraph breaks. Create or copy scenarios within the case. Each scenario references a transaction batch. Copy can create another scenario or preserve an immutable reference batch.

Status and selection

Open Edit on the scenario to choose a status: Proposal, Reviewing, Accepted, Applied or Retired. These are editable labels in this version, without transition rules or an approval workflow. Changing a label does not post entries or change the case's actual accounting contribution.

The Selected control saves your private scenario choice for that case. Other cases and other users retain their choices. Use baseline clears your private choice for the current case.

Events

Under Practitioner, open Cases and a case. Click Events or an entry count to inspect the batch for that scenario. Its description is a collapsed expander above the transactions. Edit it from the scenario row in the Case. Use the slider to switch views and expand individual transactions for details. Journal shows complete entries in date order. T-accounts shows the same lines grouped by account code in one vertical stream, with debit and credit sides, balanced totals and Balance c/f. Dates, references and draft/posted states remain visible. The orange boundary marks the partial view. Inspecting events does not change your saved scenario selection. Use browser Back to return to the scenario list and Forward to reopen Events. The existing case-name breadcrumb also returns to the scenario list. Returning rechecks scenario health.

Summary and health

Each Case opening checks every scenario. Health is independent of status. Unhealthy names are coloured and explain their findings in a tooltip. Events is disabled until reconciliation is Consistent. Events shows the supplementary summary batch first. Use the Summary–Detail switch to show one level at a time; Detail opens in consolidated T-accounts across the entire detailed batch, without summary grouping. Use Journal–T-accounts to choose its format. Summary dates show their full coverage range; detailed entries retain their individual accounting dates. Summary batches remain unselected drafts and never contribute to accounting totals. Reopen the Case to recheck after external changes.

Capture and assignment

Select a scenario and choose Use for capture on its Case. Only one Case is active for capture per user. In its selected scenario Events, Add event creates a balanced draft. Assign existing event finds a draft and previews related entries before moving them together. Posted entries and immutable or ambiguous copy-related entries cannot move. Stop capture returns ordinary new drafts to unbatched capture.

Case document

Case document downloads an HTML snapshot with justification, alternatives, scenario descriptions and the selected impact. No selection means no calculation. Administrators configure Mustache templates and transaction presentation through Lustre, User and CoA inheritance; there are no report controls.

Planning session

Select Use planning here to take ownership for this login. Other logins for the same user are refused live planning until they take it back. Selections remain unchanged. Tabs sharing a login share ownership.

Accounting in Ekwo

All accounting menu items open native Ekwo in another tab for the selected company. Native Ekwo controls its own sign-in and accounting-period selection. Trial balance, general ledger and financial statements use your saved scenario selections. Refresh the native page after changing a selection and choose a period containing the proposal dates. Direct transaction lists, matching and tax-module calculations remain on actuals. API-key-only connections cannot own a planning session. Use an accounting-only key for authoritative access.

Navigation and appearance

Fold the sidebar on desktop, or use Menu to open it on mobile. All companies returns to the company list. Open the account menu at the foot of the sidebar for the guide, accent, day/night controls and Sign out.
